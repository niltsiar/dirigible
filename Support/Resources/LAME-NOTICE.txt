Dirigible uses LAME for its MP3 export.

LAME 4.0, the libmp3lame encoder library: https://lame.sourceforge.io/
Copyright (c) the LAME authors.
License: GNU Library General Public License, version 2 or later (LGPL). The license text is in
LAME-COPYING.txt, next to this file.

Dirigible links the LAME encoder statically. It does not contain the LAME decoder (mpglib).
Dirigible changed 6 LAME files to remove compiler warnings, with no change of behavior. The source
of Dirigible, with the changed LAME files, the list of changes (Sources/CLame/README.dirigible),
and the build instructions, is at https://github.com/niltsiar/dirigible. With it, you can build
Dirigible again with a changed LAME.
