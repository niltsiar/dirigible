# 0037. Ripple edit: all tracks by default, markers follow, one shift mutator, and no ripple under a split take

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-04
- Accepted: 2026-10-04, after plan gates 1 and 2 and the implementation of run 12 (`2026-10-04-ripple-edit`).
- Amends: 0011 (the ripple commands), 0022 (the shift draw of a ripple drag), 0012 and 0021 (no ripple under a pending or split take), 0018 (Cut closes the gap, run 12 slice 3), 0020 (the Option edge drag and the lane menu, run 12 slices 2 and 4).
- Amended: 2026-10-05, run `2026-10-05-clip-set-clipboard` (see 0038). Cut of a clip set is no ripple: it removes the set clips, the gaps stay, and the playhead stays. So a pending or split take does not block it, and the lane menu shows it then. The ripple rules stay for a range.
- Amended: 2026-10-10, run 2026-10-10-studio-edit-preconditions. Studio owns the ripple check of the window (see the No ripple rule).
- Amended: 2026-10-13, run 2026-10-13-ripple-ranges (research: `docs/research/multi-range-delete.md`). `Session.rippleDelete(_ ranges:on:)` takes several ranges in one edit and one undo entry. It checks each range first. Then it merges the overlapping and touching ranges, and cuts from the last merged range to the first. The one-range form calls it.

## Context

Release 2 run 12 (`docs/requirements/releases.md` item 12) adds ripple edits (F20, F68): Ripple Delete, Ripple Cut, Ripple Insert, a ripple trim with a modifier, and a context menu on the lanes. Hindenburg PRO Cut closes the gap on the tracks of the selection, and Insert pushes the later audio on the track. Final Cut Pro ripples one spine; the other clips ride on it. Logic Pro "Cut Section Between Locators" changes all tracks, or only the selected regions. Dirigible has no spine and no connected clips, so a ripple of one track breaks the sync of a podcast with a separate track per voice. No reference app has a ripple-trim modifier. The research of the run: `docs/research/ripple-edit.md`.

## Decision

- **Scope.** A ripple changes all tracks by default. Only an explicit act limits it to the selected tracks: the two "in Selected Tracks" items of the Edit menu. A track given twice ripples once.
- **Ripple Delete.** `Session.rippleDelete(_ range:on:)` clears the range on each scope track (the edge clips split by the 0003 ID rule), then moves each clip at or after `range.upperBound` left by the length of the range.
- **Ripple Insert.** `Session.rippleInsert(_ length:at:on:)` splits the clip that holds the frame strictly inside it, then moves each clip at or after the frame right by the length. It inserts silence. A clip end past `Frames.limit` throws `.frameOutOfRange`.
- **Ripple trim.** `Session.rippleTrim(_ id:edge:by:on:)` moves one edge by a signed delta, the motion of the pointer. The span of the delta at the old edge ripples: a shorter clip is a Ripple Delete of the span, and a longer clip is an insert of silence there. The track of the clip always ripples, listed or not. The clip start stays: at the start edge, the offset changes and the audio slides under the edge, so a clip at frame 0 can extend its start. The only start limit is the file offset. The clip keeps its ID, the fade of the other edge stays and the trimmed fade yields (0035), and the crossfade at the trimmed edge goes from both clips, because the media at that join changes. The crossfade at the other edge stays, so the shortest clip is half of it. `ClipLane.ripple(at:with:)` changes the clip and moves the later clips in one step, so that crossfade keeps its join.
- **Ripple trim gesture.** Option on a trim zone (amends 0020). A drag ripples all tracks. The edge stops at the file, at a length of 1 pt or of half the crossfade at the other edge, and at `Frames.limit`, not at a neighbor. Under a pending or split take, an Option press beeps once and makes no edit. The drag moves the edge by whole device columns. With Option held over a trim zone, the cursor is `resizeLeft` at a start edge and `resizeRight` at an end edge. A flags change sets it again, and the hover path reads the keys through a seam, because `flagsChanged` reaches only the first responder. No reference app has a ripple-trim modifier; Option is the one free drag modifier.
- **Edit menu (run 12 slice 3).** Cut (Command-X) closes the gap on all tracks: the user default of U1, open for the user; Delete keeps the gap. Delete and Close Gap (Shift-Backspace) is the Ripple Delete. Insert (Command-I) moves all tracks right by the clipboard length at the playhead and pastes the clipboard there on the paste targets of 0018, in one edit. Cut in Selected Tracks (Option-Command-X) and Delete and Close Gap in Selected Tracks (Option-Shift-Backspace) are in their own group. Insert has no twin: the paste targets already set where the clipboard goes. After Cut and Delete and Close Gap, the Marks clear and the playhead goes to the range start; after Insert, the Marks are the inserted range. Under a pending or split take, each item beeps and changes nothing.
- **Lane menu (amends 0020).** A right-click or a Control-click on a lane, on a clip or in a gap, gives one menu of three groups: Cut, Copy, Paste, Insert | Delete, Delete and Close Gap, Split, Fade | Add Marker, Add Chapter. It has no Undo item and no keys, and each item runs the command of its Edit or Markers item. An item that does not apply hides; only Cut, Copy, and Paste dim. During a take only the marker group shows, and a pending or split take hides the ripple items. A right-click on a clip outside the selection selects that clip first. A Control-click starts no press.
- **Rule M (markers).** Markers follow the ripple in every scope. A Ripple Delete of R moves a marker at or after R.end left by the length of R, and a marker inside R to R.start. An insert at p moves a marker at or after p right by the length. Two markers can share a frame. Each moved marker is checked before the edit writes, so a marker past `Frames.limit` throws `.frameOutOfRange` and changes nothing. In a selected-tracks edit, the markers can drift from the tracks that did not move.
- **One edit.** Each ripple is one Core edit on a copy, so a throw changes nothing, and one undo entry.
- **Shift mutator.** `ClipLane.shift(from:by:)` moves each clip that starts at or after a frame. The joins inside the moved part stay paired, so the repair reads only the join at its edge. A crossfade that moves whole keeps its length; a crossfade at a join at R.start, at R.end, or at the insert frame goes from both clips (0036 drop rule). The new join of a ripple gets no crossfade.
- **No ripple under a pending or split take.** No edit moves the timeline under a take whose parts place at their old absolute frames apart from the parts already placed. A pending take places at its take anchor, and a split take (an unplaced segment with a sibling segment of the same take that a session file refers to) places its rest at its old frames. A wholly unplaced take, for example after an undo past a take, moves as one unit, and a placement refuses an occupied span, so it does not block a ripple. A segment whose CAF opens and reads whole but is not audio can never place at its old frames, so it does not block either; a CAF that does not open or read now (a permission, an I/O fault) can place later, so it still blocks. One predicate in DirigibleAudio, `rippleBlocked(segments:session:)` (internal), serves every entry point through two public forms: `rippleBlocked(in:session:)` for the CLI, and `Studio.canRipple`, which applies it to the disk facts of the studio, for the window, the gesture, and the lane menu. A caller declares a ripple with `ripple: true`, and Studio refuses it under a pending or split take with `StudioError.rippleBlocked`: `Studio.edit(ripple: true)`, an Insert paste, and the commit of a ripple trim drag (`GestureCommit.ripple`). The window beeps for that error.
- **CLI.** `dirigible clip ripple-delete <package> --from --to [--track]...`, `dirigible clip ripple-insert <package> --at --length [--track]...`, and `dirigible clip ripple-trim <package> <clip> --edge start|end --by <frames> [--track]...` (its own command: `clip trim` takes `--from` and `--to` as its range). No `--track` means all tracks. `--from` not under `--to` and a negative `--length` exit 64. A split take exits 3, and a `<recording>` exits 2 (0004). Ripple Cut has no command: `excerpt` does not change the package, and `clip ripple-delete` makes the edit.
- **Drag draw.** A ripple trim drag moves every later clip, so a full draw of the lanes in view (10 ms p50) and of the ruler text went over the 16 ms budget (p95 23.6 ms at fit zoom). The lanes copy the later region of the last frame by the move (amends 0022), and the ruler caches its typeset text. The drag moves the edge by whole device columns at any zoom. At a zoom that is not a whole number of frames per point, the frames of the move are rounded, so a peak or an edge of the preview can be one column off; the end of the gesture draws the lanes in full. p95 is 13.8 ms or less at fit zoom, at the 60 pt zoom, and at a fit that is not whole. A preview of only the trimmed lane stays refused, because it hides the audio that the other tracks lose.
- **Format.** No session format change. A session with no fade and no crossfade writes version 1 after a ripple.

## Consequences

- A user with an unplaced segment of a split take cannot ripple until the segment is placed (`dirigible capture place` or the banner of the window). A segment whose CAF is not audio can never place, so it does not block.
- A selected-tracks ripple moves the markers with the moved tracks, so they can drift from the tracks that did not move. The guide warns about it.
- An edit that moves the timeline later (a ripple move) must call the same predicate.

## Alternatives considered

- Markers move only in an all-tracks edit (Hindenburg, REAPER): rejected, one rule for every scope is simpler to learn, and the guide warns about the drift.
- A dialog that asks what the markers do (Logic Pro): rejected, a dialog on each edit slows the edit.
- Refuse a ripple for any unplaced segment: rejected, an undo past a take makes all its segments unplaced and no tool discards them, so Cut would stay blocked for good.
- A ripple of only the tracks of the selection by default (Hindenburg Cut): rejected, it breaks the sync of the other voices with no warning.
